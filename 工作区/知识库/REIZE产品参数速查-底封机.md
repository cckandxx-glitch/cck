## C-700 · Heat seal cold cut bag machine

> The difference between cold cut and hot cut is the edge. Hot cut seals and cuts at once; cold cut seals first and cuts after, so the edge is flatter and the mouth does not curl. Use cold cut where the bag has to look good.

- Material: LDPE/HDPE CPP/BOPP
- Film thickness: 20-100 micron
- Speed: 200 pcs/min
- Bag width: 1300 mm

URL: https://www.reizemachinery.com/products/bottom-seal-cold-cut.html

---

## CN-500 · Tension free bottom seal bag machine

> Tension free means the film is not being pulled while the bag is formed. Thin and stretchy film elongates under tension and the bag length drifts. Here the tension is released through the forming section so the length holds where it was set.

- Material: LDPE/HDPE CPP/BOPP
- Film thickness: 30-150 micron
- Speed: 180 pcs/min
- Bag width: 500 mm
- Bag length: 100-1200 mm

URL: https://www.reizemachinery.com/products/bottom-seal-tension-free.html

---

## CR-500 · Cold cut outer patch bag machine

> The patch goes on the outside of the bag, and cold cutting keeps its edge from melting. The patch is positioned by servo, forming and bonding happen on one line, and the bag can run up to 1200 mm long.

- Material: LDPE/HDPE
- Film thickness: 30-100 micron
- Speed: 150 pcs/min
- Bag width: 500 mm
- Bag length: 100-1200 mm

URL: https://www.reizemachinery.com/products/bottom-seal-outer-patch.html

## CZ-800 · Twin channel air pillow bag machine

> Air pillow bags are cushioning: high volume, low unit price, so speed is the whole story. Two channels run at once and four winding stations take turns, which is how it reaches 1800 pieces a minute.

- Material: LDPE/HDPE
- Film thickness: 20-100 micron
- Speed: 1800 pcs/min
- Bag width: 100-500 mm

URL: https://www.reizemachinery.com/products/bottom-seal-air-pillow.html

## CV-800 · Heavy duty sack machine

> A heavy duty sack carries tens of kilograms, so seal strength is everything. It takes film up to 280 micron, the knife pressure and the dwell time are set separately, and it runs up to 1000 mm wide. The modest speed is deliberate: thick film needs time in the seal.

- Material: LDPE/HDPE
- Film thickness: 50-280 micron
- Speed: 60 pcs/min
- Bag width: 300-1000 mm

URL: https://www.reizemachinery.com/products/bottom-seal-heavy-duty.html
